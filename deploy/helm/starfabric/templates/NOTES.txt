1. Port-forward the API:
   kubectl port-forward service/{{ include "starfabric.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
2. Check readiness:
   curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/readyz
3. Run reconciliation:
   sfctl reconcile

The bundled store is single-writer; replicaCount must remain 1 until an external
consensus store and leader election are configured.
